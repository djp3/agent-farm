- **Reporting to agent-grange now sends your sessions.** Turn on **Report to agent-grange** in Settings, or press **g** anywhere to turn it on and off. The sessions your patterns match go to the agent-grange on this Mac, which shows them on your team's boards. Only each session's name and state leave the Mac, with your GitHub login as their owner. A session without a name is never sent.
- Your GitHub login comes from gh's sign-in, or type it in Settings. Settings also says how the reports are going: accepted, no agent-grange running, or a grange waiting for `make update`.
- Turning reporting off takes your sessions off the boards at once. When you quit, the boards keep them, marked stale.
