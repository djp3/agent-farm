- **A band under agents at work shows how reporting to agent-grange went.** While **Report to agent-grange** is on, each hour of the last day gets a mark under its column: ● when reporting was on all hour, ◐ for part of it, a dim · when it was off. Green means the grange took your sessions, yellow that it was not running, was out of date or did not answer, red that it refused them. Beside the marks, `now` says how many sessions the last report carried, or why none went. Turning reporting off takes the band away; the hours are kept, so it comes back when you turn reporting on again. The history holds only times and answers, never session names.
- **GitHub logins with underscores work.** The logins of Enterprise Managed Users, like `mira_acme`, are now read from gh and taken in Settings, so their sessions can be reported.
- **Updates are checked more closely.** From this version on, agent-farm takes an update only from a feed signed with its own key, and checks each download's signature before unpacking it. This update arrives the usual way.
- **Closing the window quits the app**, even while the update window is open. Before, agent-farm could stay in the Dock with no window to show.
- The app now carries the licenses of Sparkle and SwiftTerm.
