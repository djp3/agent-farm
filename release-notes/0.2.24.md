Fable 5.2 now counts at its own prices, and models released after this version will count sensibly without an update.

- Fable 5.2 was counted at Fable 5's prices. That made a day on 5.2 look several times heavier than the same work on 5.1 in the daily limit use, the project graph and the `week` bar's split by model.
- A model newer than your copy of agent-farm now counts at the prices of the version before it in its family. Fable 5.3 will count like Fable 5.2 until an update says otherwise.
- Sonnet 5.5, Opus 4 and 4.1, Sonnet 3.5 and Haiku 3.5 now count at their correct prices too.
- Every model in the output-by-model bar gets a color of its own. Before, once enough models had come and gone while the app stayed open, a new one could be drawn in gray, like "Other".

The prices only decide how the week's percent is shared out. No money is shown from them.
